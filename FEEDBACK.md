# Adem

## Overall

Very positive first impression.

## Feedback

### Product Positioning

- Focus on one specific problem.
- Invoice validation before ERP import is easier to understand.
- "Business Data Quality Control" feels too broad.

---

### Homepage

- Show the Review Workspace immediately.
- Show realistic validation results.
- Visitors should instantly understand the value.

---

### Privacy

Move browser privacy much higher.

"Data stays inside your browser."

This is a strong selling point.

---

### Business Logic

Clarify whether DataPreflight validates:

- only technical issues

or

- real business logic

Examples:

- VAT consistency
- Payment terms
- Financial logic
- Date consistency

---

### Website

Keep the website small.

One excellent landing page is better than many pages.

## Product Impact

Priority:

🟢 High

Themes:

- Positioning
- Homepage
- Business Rules
- Privacy
